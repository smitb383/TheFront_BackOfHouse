## Overview
The Front is a curated retail popup store in New York City at 334 Broome St (Lower East Side). It's a gallery-style retail concept selling art, design, furniture, mirrors, lighting, textiles, and accessories from NYC-based vendors — many of whom aren't currently in other retail stores.

The team (Brooke and Del Musarra) broke from a previous project called "After Forever." The name and brand identity are confirmed as "The Front."

## Business Model
- **Commission-based**: 70/30 split with vendors (no wholesale)
- **Price range**: $15–$20 up to $1,000+; highest-priced items (furniture, mirrors, large art) serve as interior design elements
- **Revenue streams**: Commission on sales, event tickets, in-house merchandise, workshops, brand partnerships
- **POS**: Square (2.6% transaction fee)
- **Vendor selection**: Invitation and application. Curatorial criteria: craft quality, strong concept, growth potential, fits with programming theme. Rotating roster.

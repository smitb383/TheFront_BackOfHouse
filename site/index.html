01Learning from Past Pop-UpsThe Front
Which moments, products, or designers from your pop-ups created the most energy?
What sold out, got photographed, or made people linger? What did the room feel like when it was working?
What did the pop-up format prevent The Front from being — and what does a permanent space unlock?
Depth of curation, returning visitors, resident collaborators, built environments — what becomes possible with a fixed location?
Who were the people who truly understood The Front — and where do they actually gather in NYC?
The right neighborhood isn't just about rent — it's about proximity to the community you're building for.
02Concept SharpeningThe Front
How does The Front generate revenue — retail sales, event fees, designer consignment, membership, something else?
The vision is clear. The revenue model needs to be equally clear. What are the income streams and which is primary?
What's your next proof-of-concept step before committing to a permanent lease?
A longer residency, a shared space, a 3-month pop-up run — what bridges the gap between past events and a signed lease?
03Location & SpaceThe Front
Which NYC neighborhoods have you physically visited and evaluated?
LES, Nolita, Greenpoint, Bushwick, Crown Heights, Bed-Stuy — where does The Front's community already exist? What are the rent realities?
What sq footage and spatial qualities does The Front need to work as an environment, not just a store?
Ceiling height, raw quality, flexibility, visibility — The Front is immersive. What does the space need? Have you looked at specific listings?
04Money & Business PlanThe Front
What does your startup cost estimate look like — and how detailed is it?
First/last/security, build-out, fixtures, initial inventory, POS, permits, 3-month operating reserve. What's your current number — and what's still a guess?
What funding path are you pursuing — and what stage are you at?
NYCEDC programs, CDFI loans, arts grants (NYSCA, foundation grants for creative spaces), SBA microloans — which are you actively researching?
What monthly revenue does The Front need to break even — and what does hitting that require?
Rent + your pay + COGS + overhead. How many events, sales, or collaborators per month makes the math work?
05Operations & LegalThe Front
What legal and compliance steps have you completed or researched?
LLC, EIN, NYC DCA license, Certificate of Occupancy, sales tax, food handler permit if serving, event permits — what's done vs. pending?
How do you manage relationships with designers who show at The Front?
Consignment, commission, residency fees — what structure feels fair to emerging designers while keeping The Front financially viable?
What is the single most incomplete section of The Front business plan right now?
Financial projections, revenue model, competitive landscape, operations plan — name the gap and make it your focus.

NYC Arts Grant
NYC Dept of Cultural Affairs (DCLA) — Cultural Development Fund
Primary NYC arts funder. Strong fit for The Front's community programming angle. Nonprofits only — use fiscal sponsor if for-profit.
NYC Small Business
NYC Small Business Services (SBS) — Neighborhood 360°
Grants, loans, free legal + financial support for NYC small businesses. Use this regardless of whether you get funding — the free resources alone are worth it.
Arts Grant
New York State Council on the Arts (NYSCA)
State-level arts funding. Can fund for-profits via fiscal sponsorship. Apply annually once you have documented programming.
CDFI Microloan
Accompany Capital — Microloan ($500–$50k)
NYC-based CDFI. Lower bar than banks. Good for upfront costs and operating runway.
Design / Fashion
CFDA Programs + Vogue Fashion Fund
Grants for design-supporting organizations. The Front as a platform for emerging fashion designers is a strong fit. CFDA network also opens press doors.
Foundation
Jerome Foundation — Emerging Artists Program
Supports orgs creating opportunity for early-career artists. Start documenting programming history now — they want track record.
University
Parsons / The New School — Internal Grants + Alumni Programs
Seed funding for alumni ventures, Startup Studios connections. Alumni affairs office is the right first call.
SBA Microloan
SBA Microloan via Grameen America or Pursuit NYC
Up to $50k, lower interest than conventional loans.
